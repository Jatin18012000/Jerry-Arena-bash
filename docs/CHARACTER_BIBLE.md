Art direction for Phase 4: **stylised, low-poly "action figure"** fighters, built procedurally in
Blender (see [DEVELOPMENT_WORKFLOW.md](DEVELOPMENT_WORKFLOW.md#character-pipeline)) with plain
coloured materials (no textures) and rigid skinning on a shared 18-bone humanoid rig. They are
recognisable and readable at fight-camera distance, not final high-fidelity art: sculpted meshes,
textures and hand-keyed animation are a later art pass.

Preview renders: `docs/images/characters/<id>_turnaround.jpg` and `<id>_poses.jpg`
(regenerated by `build_characters.py --render`).

## Reference handling

Two reference images were supplied for Phase 4. Their visible content was studied directly and is
recorded below. Nothing here claims detail the images do not show.

> **Important — VERA's reference is an existing copyrighted character.** Reference Image A depicts
> **Juri Han from Capcom's _Street Fighter_ series**: the horn-shaped wrapped twin buns, spiked
> choker and wrist/ankle spikes, the spider-like black body markings and the specific black/purple
> outfit are her trademark design. Copying it into this game would infringe that design. VERA is
> therefore an **original character inspired by the reference's archetype and palette**. The
> deliberate differences are listed in VERA's section. Reference Image B (REX) appears to be an
> original/generic design and is followed closely.

## VERA

**Archetype:** agile kickboxer — rushdown. **Gameplay identity:** fastest walk and jump, quickest
jab, the longest low sweep in the roster; less health (950) and a short-range heavy (a knee).

### What Reference Image A shows (visible content)

| Aspect | Observed in the reference |
| --- | --- |
| Silhouette | Slim, athletic female fighter; wide, balloon-like trousers dominate the lower half; dynamic one-leg-raised fighting pose. |
| Proportions | Long legs, narrow waist, toned bare midriff and arms; heroic stylised proportions (about 7.5 heads). |
| Head / face | Oval face, sharp narrowed eyes with violet irises, defined brows, confident/smirking expression. |
| Hair | Black, blunt fringe, two tall horn-shaped buns wrapped with violet bands, a long violet streak falling across the face. |
| Clothing | White sleeveless cropped vest with violet trim over a black chest top; black baggy martial-arts trousers with violet piping; black belt with a long trailing tail. |
| Accessories | Spiked black choker; violet wrist wraps, black fingerless gloves; violet ankle wraps with teal spikes; bare feet. |
| Markings | Black spider-like markings on arms, torso and midriff. |
| Colours | Main: black, violet/magenta (~#A33CFF). Secondary: white, warm tan skin, teal accents. |
| Materials | Matte fabric (trousers, top), slightly glossy wraps/gloves, smooth skin. |
| Stance / attitude | Aggressive, playful, kick-focused. |

### What VERA's model uses (Implemented) and what was changed on purpose

| Kept from the reference (archetype / palette) | Deliberately changed (avoid copying Juri's signature design) |
| --- | --- |
| Black / violet / white palette, warm skin | Twin buns are **round**, not horn-shaped |
| Blunt black fringe, violet hair accent | Violet lock sits **at the side**, not across the face |
| White open crop vest with violet trim over a black top | **Plain violet choker**, no spikes |
| Bare midriff, toned arms | **No body markings / tattoos** |
| Black baggy trousers with violet piping, belt with trailing tail | **No teal spikes** on wraps |
| Violet wrist and ankle wraps, black fingerless gloves (violet knuckle pads), bare feet | No eye device or other signature props |

Model: ~7.2k faces, 9 materials (skin, hair, violet accent with slight emission, vest, top,
trousers, gloves, eyes, lips). Height ≈ 1.8 m.

## REX

**Archetype:** power striker. **Gameplay identity:** more health (1050), the most damage and
pushback on every button, and the longest-reaching special (a lunging straight); slower on his feet
and every move starts a frame or two later than VERA's.

### What Reference Image B shows (visible content)

| Aspect | Observed in the reference |
| --- | --- |
| Silhouette | Lean, athletic male fighter in a close-fitting suit; compact guard with fists up, one knee raised. |
| Proportions | Broad shoulders, defined chest and abs under the suit, long legs. |
| Head / face | Angular face, strong brows, focused expression; blue light marking on one cheek and small dark tech lines at the temple. |
| Hair | Black undercut: shaved sides, longer spiky top swept back. |
| Clothing | Black tech bodysuit with glowing blue piping along arms, legs and torso; short open black jacket with a high collar lined in bright blue. |
| Armour / accessories | Grey plates on shoulders, outer thighs and knees; black fingerless gloves; heavy black boots with straps; silver dragon emblem on the chest. |
| Colours | Main: black / charcoal. Secondary: electric blue (~#2B8CFF), grey armour, light skin. |
| Materials | Matte suit, slightly glossy jacket, metallic grey plates, emissive blue lines. |
| Stance / attitude | Disciplined, controlled, ready to strike. |

### What REX's model uses (Implemented)

Follows the reference: black suit with **emissive blue piping** (arms, legs, chest, jacket edges),
short **open jacket** with a **high collar, blue inside**, **grey plates** on shoulders, outer thighs
and knees, black **boots** with grey straps and soles, black **gloves** with knuckle plates,
**undercut** (dark shaved sides, black spiky top), **blue cheek marking** on the right cheek (the side
the fight camera sees), **silver chest emblem** (a plain disc — the dragon motif is a detail for a
later texture pass). ~5.3k faces, 11 materials. Height ≈ 1.8 m.

## Shared rig and animation set (Implemented)

- **Rig:** 18 bones — root, hips, spine, chest, neck, head; upperarm / forearm / hand ×2;
  thigh / shin / foot ×2 (Blender names use `.L` / `.R`; Three.js loads them as `upperarmL` etc.).
- **Clips (18):** idle, walk_forward, walk_back, crouch, jump, guard_high, guard_low, hit_high,
  hit_crouch, launched, knockdown, getup, ko, victory, attack_light, attack_heavy, attack_kick,
  attack_special.
- **Attack clips are timed to frame data**: each lasts exactly startup + active + recovery frames at
  60 fps, with the strike pose on the active frames. The game pins the clip to the combat frame, so
  the animation can never drift from the hitbox timing. Tests compare the GLB clip lengths and the
  Blender timing tables against the TypeScript move data.
